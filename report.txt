Through my work on the midterm, implementing a tradition FPS camera, which was a challenge previously, provided me the knowledge necessary to effectively tackle this. I leveraged my existing midterm code to drive the implementation of this assignment, while also taking the appropriate time to improve the actual implementation. For instance, I now support loading shaders directly from string source, not just from local file paths. I also refactored the Camera class to be much more readable.

In the past, I have implemented a camera in OpenGL. I was dissatisfied with my previous implementations, as while they worked for the specific goals of the engine, it felt to me that they were not designed properly to fulfill more broad applications. Through this assignment, I have looked back at some of my previous camera code from years ago, noting what areas should be targeted for improvement when I inevitably revisit these old projects. I am grateful for this experience and knowledge.
